## 1. The Nov 2026 scandal: Huang's own statements (Nov 8 to 12), board actions, and any reporting on an exit or a chairman/founder role

### Takeaway
On Nov 8, 2026, Huang admitted an extramarital affair in a staff email. In it he said he was "fully committed to NVIDIA" and would "earn back your confidence", and Nvidia said he would continue as CEO "without interruption." On Nov 11, the NYT reported a second relationship, with Kimberly Powell, Nvidia's VP of healthcare. Bloomberg reporting says she reported directly to him. The same day, WSJ and CBS reported that the board was preparing to oust him, and Bloomberg reported that the board had hired an external search firm and was weighing an interim CEO. As of Nov 12, I found no confirmed board decision, no public statement from Huang after Nov 8 about resigning or fighting, and no reporting that he is negotiating an exit or a move to chairman or founder.

### Inferences
- Huang's only first-person statement so far (Nov 8) is contrite but signals he intends to stay: "fully committed," "earn back your confidence." It came before the Powell report. The Powell allegation is the more dangerous one for his job, because a relationship with a direct report is a conduct and disclosure issue the board can act on without a national-security finding. The McDonald's/Easterbrook precedent (2019) applies directly.
- The board's shift from "confidence in Jensen" (Nov 8) to a statement with no confidence language (Nov 10), followed by an external CEO search (Nov 11), points to a high near-term probability of some change in role. If the WSJ, CBS and Bloomberg reports are accurate, the 1-year departure probability is now dominated by this event and is far above any age- or health-based baseline.
- Huang has no super-voting control, so he cannot block removal through his votes. As the largest individual holder and founder, however, he could plausibly be offered a non-CEO role (founder, director, adviser). Given the national-security and conduct angles, a clean break is also plausible. Neither outcome is reported.
- Nov 17 earnings and the Nov 18 congressional deadline are near-term pressure points.

### Gaps
- No confirmed board decision, resignation, interim-CEO announcement or 8-K was found as of Nov 12, 2026. Check for Nov 12 or later filings.
- I found no public statement from Huang after the Nov 11 reports, and nothing on whether he is contesting the Powell allegation.
- I found no reporting on exit negotiations, severance, clawbacks, or a chairman/founder title.
- The scope of the Wachtell/Kim investigation (whether it covers the Powell relationship) is not stated.

## 3. Huang's own pre-scandal statements on retirement, tenure and succession (condensed)

### Takeaway
For years Huang consistently signaled that he would never voluntarily retire. His statements range from "three or four decades" (2023) to "For as long as I deserve it" (Jan 2026), hoping to "die on the job" (Mar 2026), and "definitely not going to miss the next 10, 15 years" (Sept 2026, weakly sourced). He openly rejects formal succession planning.

### Cited Findings

## 4. Management philosophy relevant to succession (condensed)

### Takeaway
Huang runs Nvidia with a very flat structure: about 60 direct reports, no 1:1 meetings, no COO, and no named number two. This maximizes key-person dependence and leaves no ready internal successor.

### Cited Findings
- "My direct staff is 60 people. You know, I don't have one-on-ones with 'em because it's impossible." (Lex Fridman, Mar 2026) — [Lex Fridman transcript](https://lexfridman.com/jensen-huang-transcript)
- Nvidia has no COO, and Huang has about 60 direct reports. Nvidia has also operated without a chief compliance officer. — [Fortune, Nov 9 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/11/09/nvidia-power-built-around-jensen-huang-scandal-testing-formula/). CNBC reports internal records showing 33 direct reports in the week of Nov 9, 2026, versus the roughly 60 Huang cited in Sept 2025. — [CNBC Nov 9](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/jensen-huang-affair-nvidia-succession.html)
- Stephen Witt (author of *The Thinking Machine*): "It's just Jensen at the top... There's no second in command. There's no obvious successor." — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/if-google-wins-ai-race-nvidia-is-in-trouble-says-author-of-jensen-huang-biography-184624884.html)
- Bernstein's Rasgon: "Jensen is Nvidia. Nvidia is Jensen." Nvidia's risk factors do not name Huang as a key person. — [CNBC Nov 9](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/jensen-huang-affair-nvidia-succession.html)

### Inferences
- The flat structure means a forced exit would likely produce an interim or committee-style leadership (Futurum's "execution machine"). It also makes a transitional chairman or adviser role for Huang more tempting to a board worried about continuity.

### Gaps
- No public internal successor shortlist. The Bloomberg Jan 27, 2026 article "Nvidia Lacks Clear Successor" is paywalled and was not read.
