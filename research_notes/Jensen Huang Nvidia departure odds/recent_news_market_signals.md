# Recent News, Market Signals, and Analyst Commentary on Whether Jensen Huang Will Leave Nvidia (2025 – Nov 12, 2026)

> **CRITICAL CONTEXT FOR THE REPORT WRITER:** As of Nov 11–12, 2026, this is a live crisis. Starting about Oct 29 to Nov 8, 2026, reports emerged that Huang had an affair with a woman US intelligence reportedly believes is a Chinese Ministry of State Security (MSS) asset. She is Alice Huang, an executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore Nvidia cloud partner under a DOJ National Security Division criminal export-control probe. On Nov 8, Huang admitted to an affair in a staff memo, and the board opened an independent investigation. A securities class action was filed Nov 9, and Trump said the FBI is "looking into it." On Nov 11, the NYT reported a second relationship with a direct report, Kimberly Powell, VP of healthcare. The WSJ reported the same day that the board was "preparing to oust" him, and Bloomberg reported that the board is working with an outside firm on potential replacements. **As of my last check (2026-11-12 ~00:30 UTC = Nov 11, ~7:30 pm ET), I found no official Nvidia announcement of a departure.** Prediction markets price about 70% odds of an announcement by Nov 12 and about 90% by year-end 2026. Anything after that timestamp is not covered here.

### Inferences
- The odds moved from about 15% to about 90% (year-end 2026) in roughly 72 hours. This repricing was driven by the second-affair allegation involving a direct report, the WSJ "preparing to oust" report, and the Bloomberg search-firm report. It was not driven by the original spy-affair news alone, which markets initially shrugged off at about 15–17%.
- The Nov 12/13 contracts trade around 60–72%. This implies traders expect a decision from the Nov 11–12 board meeting within days rather than weeks.
- The Kalshi and Polymarket rules differ in ways that matter. Polymarket counts an announced future departure and a move to another Nvidia role, such as executive chairman. Kalshi requires that he "actually leave." Neither counts a leave of absence or suspension on Polymarket. A board compromise, such as Huang taking leave during the probe, would likely resolve both "No" in the short run.
- The long-dated contracts are at 96–98% (Kalshi, 2029–2033). Pre-scandal they were 30–75%, and those levels mostly reflect the near-term crisis. Before the scandal, the Nov 9 Kalshi curve (17% by 2027, 75% by 2031) is the best available "baseline" market estimate.
